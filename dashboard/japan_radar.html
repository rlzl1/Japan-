指数 = 時価総額 ÷（正味流動資産 + 賃貸等不動産の開示時価 + 非重複土地簿価 × 50%）。正味流動資産 = 現預金 + 売上債権 + 有価証券 + 投資有価証券 − 負債合計 − 非支配株主持分（棚卸資産は含めず）。不動産・土地 = 賃貸等不動産の時価（建物含む）と土地加算の合計。△はマイナス。Index = market cap ÷ (net current assets + rental real estate at disclosed fair value + 50% of land not already counted). Net current assets = cash + trade receivables + securities + investment securities − all liabilities − minority interests; inventories excluded. Tax and selling costs not deducted. Δ REF compares with the 2026-10-09 reference ranking. Not a recommendation to buy or sell.

Same rules as the Korea Filing Radar. Each category starts from a base and moves with the size of the edge: spread to the bid (≥10% +2, ≥5% +1, none −1), going private +1, a bid already raised +½, a self-tender −½; buybacks worth ≥5% of market cap +1½, ≥2% +1, off-market ToSTNeT blocks −½; dividends at ≥7% yield +1½. A stock on a value list gains a star; otherwise an RE net-net index under 0.8, net cash ≥ 0.5× market cap or a P/E ≤ 8 add ½. Losses or debt above equity cost ½ star; thin trading costs ½ (under ¥15M a day) or 1½ (under ¥3M); exchange designations (監理・特別注意) cost a star. Companies that fail the profit and debt filters are capped at two stars, except special situations, which are priced on deal terms. Bases come from the Korean 12-month backtest until the Japanese backtest replaces them. Every rating lists its reasons.

Value lists (weekly)

RE net-net (CHEAP)
Market cap ÷ (net current assets + rental real estate at fair value + 50% of non-overlapping land) below 1. The exact definition of the 不動産込みネットネット指数 ranking.
Net-nets
Current assets minus all liabilities above market cap.
Cash-rich
Cash + securities + investment securities − all liabilities ≥ 70% of market cap, profitable last year.
Deep value
P/E ≤ 6 and P/B ≤ 0.6, profitable in at least 4 of the last 5 years.
Treasury piles
Treasury shares ≥ 10% of issued at P/B ≤ 1.
Listed subsidiaries
A corporate holder at 50% or more, P/B ≤ 1: parent buy-out candidates.

Banks, brokers and insurers are left out of the asset lists. Sources: TDnet (via the yanoshin mirror), EDINET annual and semi-annual reports (rental real-estate note, major shareholders), earnings-release XBRL, Yahoo! Finance Japan prices.
